<p>This site collects proofs of basic derivative rules, derived directly from the limit definition of the derivative:</p>

<p>$$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$$</p>

<p>These pages are meant as a clean reference for students working through first-principles differentiation.</p>
