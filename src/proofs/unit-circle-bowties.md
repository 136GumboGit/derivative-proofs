<h2>The vocabulary</h2>

<h3>Bowties (2-D)</h3>

<p>Draw the four reference angles of the same size, one in each quadrant. Together they make a <b>bowtie</b> of four right triangles.</p>

<ul class="bq-vocab">
<li>All $\frac{N\pi}{6}$ angles make <b>squat</b> bowties.</li>
<li>All $\frac{N\pi}{4}$ angles make <b>equal</b> bowties.</li>
<li>All $\frac{N\pi}{3}$ angles make <b>tall</b> bowties.</li>
<li>All $\frac{N\pi}{2}$ angles land on an <b>axis</b>.</li>
</ul>

<p>The four triangles inside each bowtie are also squat, equal, or tall.</p>

<h3>Lengths (1-D)</h3>

<p>The sides of each triangle are <b>short</b>, <b>equal</b>, or <b>long</b>. On an axis, a side is <b>none</b> or <b>full</b>. All five lengths come from one pattern:</p>

<p>$$\underbrace{\sqrt{\tfrac{0}{4}}}_{\text{none}} = 0 \qquad \underbrace{\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{4}}}_{\text{short}} = \frac{1}{2} \qquad \underbrace{\sqrt{\tfrac{2}{4}}}_{\text{equal}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \qquad \underbrace{\sqrt{\tfrac{3}{4}}}_{\text{long}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \qquad \underbrace{\sqrt{\tfrac{4}{4}}}_{\text{full}} = 1$$</p>

<h3>What each function measures</h3>

<ul class="bq-vocab">
<li>$\sin\theta$ is the <b>height</b> of the point on the circle.</li>
<li>$\cos\theta$ is the distance <b>across</b> from the origin.</li>
<li>$\tan\theta$ is the <b>slope</b> of the radius, height ÷ across. A squat radius is <b>shallow</b> ($\frac{\sqrt3}{3}$), an equal one is $1$, and a tall one is <b>steep</b> ($\sqrt3$). On the x-axis the slope is $0$; on the y-axis it is undefined.</li>
</ul>

<p>The quadrant decides the sign: the height is negative below the x-axis, and the distance across is negative left of the y-axis.</p>
